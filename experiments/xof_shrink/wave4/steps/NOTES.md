# Wave 4, avenue "steps": which XOF size reductions hold for other step counts?

Configurations: 1-round Keccak XOF with S = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 steps, at log_w 4 (explore) and log_w 6
(confirm), plus S = 10 as a check for many steps. Work copy: `repo/` (base + `patch.diff`).

**How the numbers were made.**
- All sizes come from the harness code: `xofbench.py`, or its own `build_layers`/`metrics` inside
  `adv_check.py`. Frontier points were also run through `xofbench.py` itself, which checks 16 random
  messages against the variant's eager function.
- "ROBUST" means the brief's criterion was checked on this configuration: 0 wrong bits and worst
  |out/BOS - bit| <= 0.01 (float32) on all three sets:
  - `g`: the ref_gen audit, 73 messages (9 edge cases + 64 random);
  - `m`: the mixed stress set, 4 seeds x 10 = 560 messages (densities 0.005-0.995 + lane/z patterns);
  - `d`: the dense stress set, 2 x 10 = 260 messages (densities 0.85-0.99).
- Reference sets are in `refs/{g,m,d}_w<W>_s<S>.pt`, generated by `genrefs.sh` in fresh processes
  from the unmodified keccak.
- Baselines come from `$S/xof4/baselines.jsonl` (main's threshold circuits). The S = 10 baselines
  were computed here: built at log_w 4, dense from the layer shapes at log_w 6.
- Every run is one line in `results.jsonl`. `harness.jsonl` and `audits.jsonl` are the raw logs.
  `table.py W [S]` and `frontier.py W` print the tables below from them.

## 1. Headline

1. **The robust S = 3 stacks stop being robust at S = 4.**
   - Float32 errors compound from step to step, and they do so in float64 too, so they are not
     rounding.
   - Example: the robust S = 3 depth-8 point `c2:d8_rp_m4s4_mp_cp_u1_sl_g_p1dct_ra` on more steps
     (log_w 4, same builder, `steps:d8g`):

     | S | worst error | result |
     |---|---|---|
     | 3 | 5.1e-3 | passes |
     | 4 | 3.8e-2 (mixed set) | fails |
     | 6 | 0.52 (1 wrong bit); 0.30 in float64 | fails |
     | 10 | 4.4e4 (7713 wrong bits) | fails |

   - Sharper silu is worse: q = 16 or 32 raises the error, and no q fixes it.
2. **Cause.** Forms whose knots do not damp small errors, and whose errors every later round then
   amplifies:
   - column-packing decoders (`cp`);
   - irrational-knot D forms in middle X layers (`p1dc`, the sp `p1d`);
   - min-parity on middle-step counts (`mpc` in direct middles);
   - round-1 u pairs (`u1`).

   Forms whose errors meet few later rounds stay safe:
   - round-1 raw-bit forms (`mp`, `ra`);
   - every last-round trick (lazy chi, fold, c5, bt, sl, s4).

   At S = 6-10 the weak spot is the late digests. The digest read through cp's decoders is packed 4
   bits per feature, and the lazy digest is packed as F = o1 + 5 o2. Both multiply its analog error.
3. **What keeps the dense savings robust as S grows.**
   - Allow only as many cp X layers as the error budget holds:

     | S | cp X layers | condition |
     |---|---|---|
     | 3 | 1 (all) | |
     | 4 | 2 (all) | without u1 |
     | 6 | the last 2 (`cp2`) | late digests carried as pairs (`lp2`, new) |
     | 10 | the last 1 (`cpl`) | `lp2`, and without lz5; tight at log_w 6 (9.0e-3) |

   - Keep p1dc only in the last X (`p1dcl`).
   - Keep everything else.

   Robust dense-best points at log_w 6 (float32, 3 sets):

   | S | depth | variant | dense | vs baseline |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 4 | 11 | `x_d8_m4_s4_mp_cp_sl_p1dcl_ra` | 78.2M | 58.0x |
   | 6 | 17 | `x_d8_m4_s4_mp_cp2_sl_p1dcl_ra_lp2` | 162.8M | 44.6x |

   At S = 10, `x_d8_m4_s4_mp_cpl_sl_p1dc_ra_lp2` is 37.7x at log_w 6 (369.6M; tight, 9.0e-3 on the
   mixed set) and 37.6x at log_w 4.
   With only bfloat16-exact forms (`steps:x_d8_m4_s4_sl`: glu_xor split steps, lazy4 + fold, m4s4,
   sl, plus `lp2` at S = 10), the circuit is float32-robust **and correct with bf16 weights** (w16) on
   all three sets. At log_w 6:

   | S | 2 | 4 | 6 | 10 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | x baseline | 88.3 | 47.2 | 40.3 | 36.6 |

   That is 26% down to 3% more dense than the float32 best. The sparse end is `sp:col1b`
   at every S: best margins (1e-4), 18-27x sparse, and bf16-weight-exact (w16-correct at log_w 6,
   S = 6).
4. **Cost of an extra step** (robust stacks, log_w 4):
   - +3 layers;
   - +2.09M of computation, constant. The same step with cp in its X is 1.54M, so robustness costs
     26% per middle step;
   - +12.5K sparse;
   - carries whose increment grows linearly: carry ≈ 0.06M x S^2, which is 15% of dense at S = 3-4,
     17-18% at S = 6 and 25-26% at S = 10.

   At log_w 6 an extra step costs 38.8-42.5M against the baseline's 1.23-1.37B, so about 32x.
   The dense ratio to the baseline therefore falls with S:

   | S | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 10 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | x baseline | 386 | 111 | 75 | 58 | 44.6 | 37.7 |

   It tends toward the per-step ratio, then below it once the S^2 carries dominate, near S ~ 35 (an
   estimate from the fit).
5. **Best pattern for many steps:**
   - u1-free split round 1;
   - middle steps split (chi | X | Y), with unpacked chi bits and glu_xor D;
   - cp + p1dc only in the last one or two X layers;
   - lazy4 + fold or lazy4c + c5 + bt + sl at the end;
   - digests m4 through the middle, split to pairs at the last theta (`m4s4`), with the late digests
     as pairs (`lp2`).

   At S = 10, log_w 6 the stacks with round-1 raw-bit forms sit at 8-9e-3. The widest margin there
   comes from bf16-exact forms plus `lp2`: `x_d8_m4_s4_sl_lp2`, 381.1M (36.6x) at 3.8e-3.

   Direct middle steps cost 1.4-1.5x more per step, and packing beyond 4 bits does not pay.

Variants are named `steps:x_<layout>_<flags>` (module `__getattr__`), flags as in c2 (`m<j>`, `s4`,
`sl`, `c5`, `lz5`, `mp`, `mpc`, `ra`, `bt`, `u1`, `cp`, `p1dc`, ...) plus `cpl` (cp only in the last X),
`cp<j>` (last j X), `p1dcl`, `lp<j>` (late digests as pairs), `walsh`, `nc` (NOCARRY). `steps:d8g`, `d7g`, `d6g` are the robust S = 3
flag stacks on the S-step layouts; at S = 3 they reproduce the c2 points exactly (log_w 4: 2,758,126 /
3,047,047 / 3,325,896), and the generalized `sp`, `xs4`, `dl3` reproduce their S = 3 sizes exactly.

**Why every new construction is exact.** Every circuit is also checked against its own eager
function (harness) and against the unpatched reference xof (audits).
- **Existing exact pieces only.** The S-step layouts choose, per step, among pieces that are already
  exact: split, direct, lazy4, lazy4c, the fold, sp's rounds, xs4 / dl3's lazy rounds.
  - Each piece reads the previous step's chi or its counts through the same interfaces as at S = 3.
  - A lazy round reads counts of o' in {0, 1, 2}. Their range is <= 21 for any input step, and the
    scale 32 keeps the features <= 1, so the round can repeat.
- **Subsets of exact tricks.** `cpl` / `cp<j>` / `p1dcl` apply cp and the p1dc D to a subset of the
  X layers. Each X is exact with or without them.
- **`lp<j>`.** It packs some digests as pairs p = a + 2b, with the existing exact pair decoders,
  instead of 4 bits per feature.
- **`dl3:w1`** uses chi(a, b, c) = (p(A) - p(A xor B) + p(A xor C) + p(A xor B xor C)) / 2, checked on
  all 8 inputs. The raw-bit sets A, B, C belong to the three theta bits. Each p() is an exact parity
  form on raw message bits (`raw_par`), and the combination is linear, in wo.
- **`NOCARRY`** is a measurement only. It changes the output (last digest only), and the harness
  verifies it against that eager function.

## 3. Float32 errors compound over steps: which strategies break

### 3.1 The S = 3 robust stacks at more steps (log_w 4, worst |out/BOS - bit|)

| stack (S = 3 robust point on the S-step layout) | S = 2 | S = 3 | S = 4 | S = 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d8 `steps:d8g` = `c2:d8_rp_m4s4_mp_cp_u1_sl_g_p1dct_ra` | 1.3e-3 (harness) | 5.1e-3 (g/m/d) | **3.8e-2** (m) | **0.52** (g, 1 wrong bit) |
| d7 `steps:d7g` | 1.8e-3 (g/m/d) | 5.1e-3 | **2.7e-2** (m) | **0.45** (g) |
| d6 `steps:d6g` | 1.2e-3 (g/m/d) | 3.4e-3 | **1.9e-2** (m) | **0.36** (g) |
| sparse end `sp:col1b` (no p1d forms) | 1.9e-5 | -- | 7.5e-5 | 1.6e-4 |

**Not rounding.** Take `steps:d8g` at S = 6 and run it in float64 with the same weights (`diag.py`). The
worst error per digest is 6.5e-4, 4.1e-3, 3.6e-2, 8.9e-2, 3.8e-2 and 0.30 for digests 1-6; float32 gives
1.3e-3 ... 0.50.

**Sharper gates make it worse.** On the S = 6 audit (`adv_check --q`):

| q | 4 | 6 | 8 | 16 | 32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| worst error | 1.12 | 0.82 | 0.52 | 0.67 | 0.94 |
| wrong bits | 302 | 13 | 1 | 6 | 22 |

**Mechanism.**
- A gated unit max(0, g) v passes an input error with its local slope.
- silu smoothing damps an error only at a V-shaped knot that sits on a lattice point, such as
  glu_xor at even counts or xor2 at a + b = 0. There the smoothed kink has zero mean slope, so the
  error comes out quadratically, about k e^2 / 2 with k = c q = 32. That holds only while e < 1/k.
- Knots of other kinds carry the error forward linearly, often with a gain above 1:
  - one-sided hinges on the lattice, such as the cp decoder relu(p - 1)(4 - p)/2 with slope 3/2;
  - knots between integers (min-parity);
  - irrational knots (the p1d forms).
- Every Keccak round sums 11 of these errors into a theta count and passes them on through chi's
  slopes. Errors from the middle steps therefore grow geometrically with the number of steps that
  follow, and larger q shrinks the damped region.

## 4. Robust frontier per step count

**How the rows are chosen.**
- Every row passed all three float32 sets at that log_w and S: `g` 73 messages, `m` 560, `d` 260.
- Each row is the best dense at its depth, and a depth is dropped when a shallower row is already
  smaller. The last row of each block is the sparse end.
- Ratios are baseline / ours, from `baselines.jsonl`. The S = 10 baselines were computed here: the
  log_w 4 one is built, and the log_w 6 dense one comes from the layer shapes (`basedense.py`), with
  its sparse count unknown.
- The "harness" column is `xofbench.py` on 16 random messages.
- "w16" is `bf16_check` with weights rounded to bfloat16 and float32 activations, on the sets listed.
- S = 3 at log_w 6 is the lead's frontier (brief), not re-run here. At log_w 4 the S = 3 points were
  re-checked on these sets.

## 6. Best layout pattern for many steps

**Measured choices** (log_w 4 unless stated).
- **Middle steps: split, with unpacked chi bits and glu_xor D.** A split step (chi | X | Y) adds 3
  layers and 2.09M of computation.
  - A direct middle step (chi -> counts | theta) adds 2 layers but costs more. Only glu_xor thetas are
    robust (min-parity `mpc` thetas break). With them `x_dN` costs 4.07M per step against 2.67M for
    split, carries included (S = 4 -> 6: 7.20M -> 15.35M against 5.76M -> 11.10M).
  - Direct middles also carry more per step, since their layers are wider.
  - They only trade size for depth: at S = 6, log_w 6, depths 12 / 13 / 14 / 15 cost 245.6M /
    223.9M / 201.7M / 177.7M.
- **cp (with the p1dc D) only in the last X layers, as many as the error budget holds** (section 3.3):
  - all X at S <= 4 (without u1);
  - the last 2 at S = 6 (with lp2);
  - the last 1 at S = 10 (with lp2, without lz5).

  cp is worth 0.55M per X at log_w 4, so this recovers a constant 0.5-1.1M.
- **Late digests as pairs (`lp2`, new).** The digest made in the last cp X is read through cp's
  decoders and was then packed 4 bits per feature, which multiplies its error. Carrying it as pairs
  costs +0.07M and is what lets cp2 (S = 6) and cpl (S = 10) pass.
  - For the same reason, drop lz5 (the lazy digest as F = o1 + 5 o2) at large S.
  - The fold layout (d8: lazy4 + parity of range 11) has the largest margins at S = 10.
- **Round 1:**
  - split without u1 (d8/d7) or direct with the raw-bit forms `ra` + `mp` (d6);
  - u1 fails with cp at S >= 4 at log_w 6 (1.23e-2 with cpl, 2.8e-2 with cp + lp2).
  - The round-1 theta pool (`tp`/`tr`) was not tried: it is float32-tight at S = 3 already, and
    round-1 errors meet every later round.
- **End:** lazy4 + fold (d8) or lazy4c + c5 + bt + sl (d6/d7). All are robust, but they save a
  constant amount, so their share falls as 1/S.
- **Digests:**
  - m4 through the middle, split into pairs at the last theta (`m4s4`), late digests in pairs (`lp2`);
  - m5/m6 cost more in decoders than they save in carries, and m8 fails float32.

**Proposed pattern for S steps** (depth 3S - 1), `x_d8_m4_s4_mp_cp<j>_sl_p1dcl_ra_lp2`:

    X1 | Y1                                  split round 1 with raw-bit forms (mp, ra), no u1
    [chi | X | Y] x (S - 2 - j)               middle steps: unpacked chi bits, glu_xor D
    [chi (cp) | X (cp, p1dc D) | Y] ...       the last j middle X layers with cp (j = 2 at S <= 6,
                                              1 at S = 10)
    lazy chi (lazy4) | fold | theta (sl) | chi
    digests: m4 through the middle, pairs at the last theta (s4), the last packed digest in pairs (lp2)

- **If the weights must be bfloat16** (w16), drop the non-bf16-exact forms: mp, ra, cp, p1dc, u1,
  mpc, and lazy4c's 1/3 column reduction. Use `x_d8_m4_s4_sl` (+ `lp2` from S ~ 10). It is
  float32-robust and w16-correct at log_w 6 for S = 2 / 4 / 6 / 10 (88.3 / 47.2 / 40.3 / 36.6x), which
  is 26% down to 3% larger than the float32 best. At S = 10, log_w 6 it has the widest float32 margin
  of the dense stacks (3.8e-3).
- For depth 3S - 3 use the d6 version: direct round 1 with `ra`, lazy4c + c5 + bt at the end, no
  lz5. It is 5% larger at S = 6 (log_w 4) and 11% larger at S = 4 (log_w 6).
- Per extra step: +3 layers, +2.09M of computation (log_w 4) or +33.3M (log_w 6), plus carries of
  about 0.06M x (2S - 1) (log_w 4).

**Where the S = 10 error sits** (`digerr.py`: worst float32 error per digest, mixed set).
- `x_d8_..._cpl`: digests 1-10 give 6.9e-4, 5.6e-4, 1.5e-3, 1.9e-3, 9.6e-4, 2.0e-3, 1.9e-3,

- `x_d6_..._cpl_..._lz5`: 3.3e-4 ... 2.2e-3, then **6.9e-3** (digest 8) and **1.2e-2** (digest 9, the
  lazy digest in lz5).
- `cp2` at S = 6 fails the same way: digest 4 gives 1.1e-2, and the last digest only 2.9e-3.

So what breaks is not the last round. It is the **late digests**:
- the digest made in the cp X is read through cp's decoders, then packed 4 bits per feature, which
  multiplies its analog error by up to 15 in p units;
- the lazy digest in lz5 (F = o1 + 5 o2, a 25-level decoder) behaves the same way.

**Fix: `lp<j>`, pack by age.** The last packed digests cross only a few layers, so carry them as
pairs (lp2 = the last m4 digest before the lazy one), and drop lz5. This costs +0.07M at S = 6 and
S = 10, and it brings cpl back at S = 10.

**Carries at S = 10:**
- `x_d6_..._cpl` no-carry 17,499,710 against 23,702,917: carries are 6.2M (26%);
- `x_d8_..._cpl` no-carry 17,224,868 against 23,109,659: 5.9M (25%).
- The computation per step stays 2,093,781 (no-carry S = 6 -> 10: 9,124,586 -> 17,499,710 = 4 x
  2,093,781).
- The carry increment per step grows: 1.06M per step for S = 6 -> 10. The carry is about 0.06M x S^2
  at log_w 4, so by extrapolation (an estimate) it overtakes the computation near S = 35.

## 9. Remaining ideas

- **A damping cp decoder.** Knots on half-integers make the decoders flat at every lattice point:
  - hi bit of p in {0..3}: max(0, p - 1.5)(10 - 4p) + 8 max(0, p - 2.5), 2 units;
  - [E == 1] on E in {0, 1, 2}: max(0, E - 0.5)(6 - 4E) + max(0, E - 1.5)(4E - 2), 2 units.
  Both are exact, and they reset errors instead of passing them on. They cost about 2x the decoder
  units, while cp saves 26% of a middle step (0.55M of 2.09M at log_w 4). A damped cp (or a damped Y
  every k steps) might let cp back into the middle steps at a net gain. Not built.
- **Periodic error resets.** One damped layer every few steps: re-threshold the chi bits, or a damped
  Y. It would buy error budget for the non-damping tricks (cp, u1, p1dc) at a known cost per step.
- **Carry packing by age, both ways.**
  - `lp<j>` (late digests as pairs) is built and needed. At S = 10, log_w 6 it moves the bf16-exact
    stack from 1.03e-2 to 3.8e-3 on the mixed set.
  - The other direction is not built: pack the oldest digests tighter. Merge two m4 features into
    one m8 feature at emission (one wo row), and split them back in stages near the end
    (m8 -> m4 -> pairs, as s4 does).
  - m5+ decoded directly does not pay (section 5), and m8 fails float32 (0.11). Staged splits would
    only pay once the carries dominate, near S ~ 35 at log_w 4 (an estimate).
- **Shallow layouts at S >= 6 and log_w 6.** They run (depth S, S + 1, S + 2) but are 4-10x larger
  than the split layouts. At S = 4, log_w 6 only the plain `dl3:d4x` (depth 5) passes, and S = 6 was
  audited at log_w 4 only.
- **Round-1 theta pool (tp / tr) at S != 3,** with the damping argument in mind: its errors enter
  before every later step, so it probably fails for S >= 4 at log_w 6.
